## 📁 How to place your files

When downloading or pushing to your repository:
1. Drag or copy your 12 `.webp` image files into this directory: `public/images/banyan/`.
2. Either name style (`banyan-amenity-01.webp` or `Waikiki Banyan T2 Building and Ammenities (1).webp`) will automatically be recognized and displayed by the website.
